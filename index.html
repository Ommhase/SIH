Methodology

From observation to nowcast.

A 4-step scientific workflow turning dense observational streams into clear operational early warnings.

01 — OBSERVE

Collect atmospheric signals

Ingest Doppler radar reflectivity sweeps, geostationary satellite IR cooling rates, lightning strike counts, and numerical fields.

02 — FUSE

Harmonize tensor fields

Construct spatiotemporal tensors uniting reflectivity grid cells, CAPE parcel buoyancy, and moisture boundary convergence.

03 — PREDICT

Estimate short-term storm risk

04 — EXPLAIN

Deliver clear intelligence

Present intuitive geographic maps, plain-language explanations, and sector-specific advisories that teams can act upon immediately.

About SKYBOLT AI

Making complex atmospheric intelligence simple and actionable.

SKYBOLT AI is an AI-assisted atmospheric intelligence platform focused on short-term thunderstorm and lightning nowcasting.

Severe convective thunderstorms and sudden lightning strikes present grave risks to human life, aviation safety, agricultural harvesting, and power utility distribution. By synthesizing multi-source observational signals in real time, our mission is to deliver life-saving early warning lead times with clarity, confidence, and actionable precision.

Scientific Sources & Methodology

The mathematical and spatiotemporal algorithms powering SKYBOLT AI are calibrated according to peer-reviewed convective nowcasting literature:

  • Mostajabi et al. (2019): Nowcasting lightning occurrence from meteorological parameters using machine learning. Nature Climate & Atmospheric Science.
  • Shi et al. (2015, 2017): Convolutional LSTM Network: A Machine Learning Approach for Precipitation Nowcasting. NeurIPS.
  • World Meteorological Organization (WMO): Guidelines on Nowcasting Techniques and High-Impact Weather Early Warnings.
  • Observational Feeds: Open-Meteo Global Forecasting, Doppler Weather Radar Reflectivity Grids, Geostationary Satellite Infrared Radiometers.
